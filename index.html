The complete bulk ecosystem – how we power your business

fc-exports is not just another wholesale supplier. We are a full-service bulk purchase and sales partner based in the USA. Our model is simple: you order large quantities from us, and we take care of the rest – from warehousing to final delivery, and even selling your products across American markets. Whether you are a retailer, e‑commerce brand, or distributor, we eliminate the complexity of cross‑border logistics and sales.

What makes us different?

Most wholesalers only supply goods. We go far beyond that. When you buy bulk from fc-exports, you are not just getting products – you are getting a turnkey sales engine. We maintain a robust network of B2B and B2C channels in the USA, so your inventory moves fast. Our team manages everything: sourcing, quality checks, storage, fulfillment, and even marketing support. You focus on your core business; we focus on selling your bulk orders.

How the process works in detail

  • Step 1 – Bulk order placement: You select from our curated product list (or request custom sourcing). We offer competitive wholesale prices because we purchase directly from American manufacturers and importers.
  • Step 2 – Warehousing & inventory management: Your entire bulk shipment is stored in our secure, climate‑controlled warehouses across the USA. You get real‑time visibility of your stock.
  • Step 3 – Multi‑channel selling: We list and promote your products through our sales channels – online marketplaces, retail partnerships, and direct B2B outreach. We act as your extended sales team.
  • Step 4 – Order fulfillment & shipping: Every order placed by your customers is packed and shipped from our US warehouses within 24‑48 hours. We handle returns and customer service, too.
  • Step 5 – Profit & growth: You receive regular reports and payouts. Our wholesale margins are transparent, and we continuously optimize pricing to maximize your ROI.

Why this model works for USA sellers

The American market demands speed, reliability, and local presence. By partnering with fc-exports, you bypass the headaches of international shipping, customs, and warehousing. Your products are already inside the USA, ready to be sold. We also provide market intelligence – what sells, at what price, and in which region. This data‑driven approach ensures your bulk inventory is never stagnant.

Who is this for?

Transparent pricing & wholesale margins

We believe in fair, upfront pricing. Our bulk rates are among the most competitive in the industry, because we buy at scale and pass the savings to you. There are no hidden fees – you see the cost per unit, the selling price, and your net profit margin. We also offer volume‑based discounts for larger orders. Our goal is to make wholesale profitable for you, so we work hard to drive sales.

Full‑cycle support – from purchase to final sale

Unlike traditional wholesalers, we don't disappear after delivery. We stay with you through the entire lifecycle of your bulk order. Our team provides marketing collateral, product photography, and listing optimization to help your products stand out. We also handle customer inquiries, returns, and replacements – so you never have to deal with end‑user issues. This is what we mean by “you purchase, we sell” – a true partnership.

Ready to scale your business? Whether you are new to bulk purchasing or an experienced seller, fc-exports offers a seamless, profitable path to the USA market. We handle the heavy lifting – you enjoy the returns. Our integrated approach (purchase + storage + selling) is the simplest way to turn bulk inventory into consistent revenue.
