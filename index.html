The Complete Wholesale Growth Partnership

FCExport (FaizaCraft Export) is an India-based manufacturer and exporter of handcrafted antique, vintage and decorative products.

We work with retailers, online sellers, importers, distributors and entrepreneurs who want to purchase unique products in bulk and sell them in their own markets.

Our model is simple: You purchase products from us in bulk, we manufacture and export them, and we support you with product and digital marketing knowledge.

What Makes FCExport Different?

Many suppliers focus only on manufacturing and shipping products. We believe a stronger wholesale relationship can offer more value.

Our team also has experience in Google Ads, digital marketing, online advertising and customer acquisition. Because of this, we understand not only how to source products but also how products can be presented and promoted online.

We can share practical ideas with our wholesale partners around product selection, product positioning, online advertising and digital marketing.

How the Wholesale Process Works

  • Choose your products: Browse our catalog or tell us what type of products you are looking for.
  • Discuss quantities: Tell us your required quantity, target market and product requirements.
  • Place your bulk order: Get wholesale pricing based on your order quantity and requirements.
  • Manufacturing & quality: We manufacture and prepare the products according to the agreed specifications.
  • Export: We prepare the order for international shipment and handle the export process from India.
  • You sell in your market: Sell through your website, marketplaces, retail stores, distributors or other channels.
  • We help you grow: Our digital marketing and Google Ads experience can help you understand online promotion and customer acquisition.

Why This Model Works

A successful product business needs more than a good supplier. You need the right product, competitive pricing, attractive presentation, traffic and customers.

FCExport can support you on the product side while also bringing digital marketing knowledge to the partnership.

This gives our wholesale partners an opportunity to build a complete product business instead of simply purchasing products from a supplier.

Digital Marketing & Google Ads Support

Online selling can be difficult when you do not know how to reach the right customers.

Our experience with Google Ads and digital marketing allows us to understand how online traffic, advertising and product positioning work together.

Depending on the partnership, we can share ideas around Google Ads campaigns, product positioning, landing pages, advertising strategy and customer acquisition.
